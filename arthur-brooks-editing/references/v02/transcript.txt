You have to model exactly what you want to see. If you don't want your kids to be screwing around with their phones at dinner, you put your phone away and you're not looking at it and you're having a conversation with them at dinner. And it won't work right away, but that model really works. If you want your kids to practice the faith, you must practice the faith. If you don't want your kids to drink, you shouldn't drink. If you don't want your kids to be abusive, yelling taunts out the car window in traffic, you better never do that.
