Most people assume that happiness and unhappiness are opposites. If you have more of one, you have less of the other. It's on a dial, basically. But that's wrong. In truth, happiness and unhappiness are separate systems. Researchers confirmed this in 1988 with a tool called PANIS, the Positive and Negative Affect Schedule. You get two independent scores. Your positive affect and your negative affect. You can run high on both. You can run low on both. Most people have spent their entire lives trying to manage one dial. They didn't know they had and completely ignoring the other. Mother Nature built it this way on purpose. Negative emotions are a survival system. They keep you scanning for threats. Positive emotions push you toward opportunity. Those are two different systems. The ancients understood this intuitively. Ecclesiastes speaks of a time to weep and a time to laugh, both belonging to a full life. My negative dial runs pretty hot. For years, I assumed that meant something was wrong with me, but it doesn't. It means I have a specific setting and it's not a malfunction. And knowing that changed what I was actually working on day to day. I stopped trying to manufacture cheerfulness and I started managing the negative dial through sleep, exercise, and food. Not turning it to zero, but managing it. That's the unglamorous machinery of a good life. The wellness industry will sell you the idea that feeling better and feeling less bad are the same project. They're not the same project. And if you try to eliminate your unhappiness entirely, you will eliminate your happiness with it. So the first step is knowing your positive and negative affect numbers. Not to fix them, to understand what you're exactly working with, and to make the correct changes. You can't be happy, but you can be happier, but not by turning the wrong dial.
